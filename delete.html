Data Deletion

Pouf: BG & Object Remover — provided by RY Mobile Software

Pouf does not use accounts and does not know your name or email address. Your data is linked only to a random anonymous identifier stored on your device. This page explains how to have that data deleted.

How to Request Deletion

1. If you have an active subscription, cancel it first in the Google Play Store (Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions). Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription.

2. Send an email to rukiyesayar1810@gmail.com with the subject "Pouf data deletion request".

3. If you have ever subscribed, include your Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA."). You can find it in your Google Play receipt email or in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions > Budget & history. This is the only way we can find your record.

We will complete your request within 30 days and confirm by email.

If You Never Subscribed

Uninstall the app or clear its data (Settings > Apps > Pouf > Storage > Clear data). This removes the anonymous identifier from your device. The remaining usage counters can no longer be linked to you or your device.

What Is Deleted

The usage record linked to your anonymous identifier in our database: your plan, usage counters, and subscription status.

Your customer record at RevenueCat, our subscription management provider.

What Is Not Kept or Is Kept Separately

Photos: we do not store your photos. Temporary copies created during processing are deleted automatically within one hour.

Server logs: technical logs are deleted automatically, typically within 30 days.

Google Play purchase records: Google keeps its own records of your purchases under Google's privacy policy. We cannot delete these.
