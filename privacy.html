Privacy Policy

Effective August 22, 2026

This policy explains what Pouf does with your data. It applies to the Pouf mobile app provided by RY Mobile Software.

Summary

Pouf does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number. The only data we handle is the photo you choose to edit, and it is used solely to produce the edited result you asked for.

Photos You Edit

When you select a photo, the app resizes it on your device and sends it to our processing server over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Our server forwards the photo to third-party AI providers that perform the actual editing (background removal, object removal, and object detection). The processed result is returned to your device.

Photos are processed only to fulfil your request. They are not used to train AI models by us, and they are not shared with advertisers or sold to anyone.

The edited result is stored only on your device, in the app cache and — if you tap Save — in your gallery. Clearing the app data or uninstalling removes the cached files.

Third-Party Processing

Image editing is carried out by external AI services. When your photo is sent for processing, it is handled by those providers under their own privacy terms.

Our server infrastructure is provided by Google Cloud / Firebase.

These providers process your photo only to return the edited result and act on our instructions.

Permissions We Request

Photos and media: needed so you can choose a photo to edit and save the result back to your gallery. The app can only see the photos you explicitly select.

Internet: needed to send the photo for processing and receive the result.

We do not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, or location.

We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not use advertising identifiers.

We do not create a user profile or link edits to your identity.

Children

Pouf is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict its processing.

Because the app does not create accounts and does not retain your photos after processing, we typically hold no personal data about you. If you have a request, contact us at the address below.

Security

All communication between the app and our servers uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to our processing infrastructure is restricted.

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, announced in the app.
